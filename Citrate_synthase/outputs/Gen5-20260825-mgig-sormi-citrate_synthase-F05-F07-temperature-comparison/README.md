Outputs generated by
[`../../code/Gen5-20260825-mgig-sormi-citrate_synthase-F05-F07-temperature-comparison.Rmd`](../../code/Gen5-20260825-mgig-sormi-citrate_synthase-F05-F07-temperature-comparison.Rmd).

Cross-plate comparison of citrate synthase activity in *M. gigas* ctenidia
between families `F05` and `F07` at ambient and 36 &deg;C. This analysis does
not process plate-reader files; its inputs are the
`citrate_synthase_activity_results.csv` outputs of three per-plate analyses:

- `../Gen5-20260824-mgig-sormi-citrate_synthase-F05-ambient/`
- `../Gen5-20260814-mgig-citrate_synthase-F05-36C/`
- `../Gen5-20260824-mgig-sormi-citrate_synthase-F07-ambient_and_36C/`

**Two conventions differ from the per-plate analyses:**

1. The 2026-08-11 `F05`-ambient plate is **excluded** (it was repeated on
   2026-08-24 because of poor sample and standard-curve quality).
2. Background luminescence is **not subtracted** from any activity value,
   because the `F07` plate has no estimable background (Reaction Mix was
   added to its background wells in place of Background Control Mix). All
   four groups are therefore treated identically.

## Files

- [`cs_activity_all_families_no_background.csv`](cs_activity_all_families_no_background.csv):
  All 32 individuals (8 per family &times; temperature cell), with background
  subtraction removed.

    | Column | Description |
    |--------|-------------|
    | `Sample` | Sample name (e.g. `F05_01_36C`) |
    | `Family` | Family (`F05` or `F07`) |
    | `Individual` | Individual number within the family/temperature group |
    | `Temperature` | Exposure (`ambient` or `36C`) |
    | `Protein (mg)` | Total extracted protein, carried through from the 2026-08-13 BSA analyses |
    | `Raw rate (mA412/min)` | Reaction rate before any background subtraction |
    | `Activity, no BG (mU/mg)` | Activity used throughout this analysis -- background **not** subtracted |
    | `Activity, published (mU/mg)` | Background-corrected activity as reported by the per-plate analysis, for reference |
    | `Reps used` | Technical replicates retained by the per-plate analysis (e.g. `3/3`) |
    | `CV used (%)` | CV across the retained replicates |
    | `QC` | Per-plate QC verdict for the individual |

- [`tukey_pairwise_contrasts.csv`](tukey_pairwise_contrasts.csv): Tukey HSD
  contrasts between all four family &times; temperature groups, from the
  two-way ANOVA on log<sub>10</sub> activity. Log-scale differences are
  back-transformed to fold changes with 95% confidence intervals.

- [`cs_activity_by_family_temperature.png`](cs_activity_by_family_temperature.png):
  **The primary result figure.** Box plots of all four groups on one axis
  (median, IQR, whiskers to 1.5 &times; IQR) with every individual overlaid.
  Significance brackets are generated from the Tukey HSD table rather than
  entered by hand, and cover the four contrasts differing in exactly one
  factor -- the two within-family temperature contrasts and the two
  within-temperature family contrasts. The two "diagonal" contrasts, which
  change family and temperature simultaneously, are not annotated but remain
  in `tukey_pairwise_contrasts.csv`.

- [`cs_activity_interaction_plot.png`](cs_activity_interaction_plot.png):
  Group means &plusmn;95% CI joined by family. Non-parallel lines are the
  family &times; temperature interaction.

- [`cs_activity_per_individual.png`](cs_activity_per_individual.png):
  Per-individual activity ranked low to high, colour-coded by family and
  shape-coded by temperature.

- [`background_correction_effect.png`](background_correction_effect.png):
  Activity without background subtraction against the originally published
  background-corrected activity, with a 1:1 reference line -- shows the size
  of the convention change described above.

- [`anova_residuals_vs_fitted.png`](anova_residuals_vs_fitted.png) and
  [`anova_qq_residuals.png`](anova_qq_residuals.png): Residual diagnostics for
  the two-way ANOVA, colour- and shape-coded by group.

## Figure colours

All figures use the **Okabe-Ito** colourblind-safe qualitative palette, which
remains distinguishable under deuteranopia, protanopia and tritanopia. One hue
is bound to each family (`F05` blue `#0072B2`, `F07` vermillion `#D55E00`) and
reused across every figure; the 36 &deg;C member of each family pair is drawn
in the lighter tint of that hue (`#56B4E9`, `#E69F00`). Temperature is
redundantly encoded by point shape, so no figure depends on colour as its only
channel.
